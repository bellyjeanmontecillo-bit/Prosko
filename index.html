Unused SK Youth Budget: Year-End Policy & Legal Guidance

Compliance with RA 10742 as amended by RA 11768 & COA Circular No. 2020-004

1. SK Fiscal Autonomy

Under RA 11768, unused SK funds DO NOT revert to the mother Barangay Council or City/Municipal treasury. 100% of unused balances remain inside the dedicated SK bank depository account.

2. Continuing Appropriations

Unexpended capital outlay budgets for ongoing youth projects carry over as Continuing Appropriations valid for up to three (3) calendar years.

3. Re-Appropriation via ABYIP

General unexpended balances accrue to the SK Trust Fund / Unappropriated Surplus on December 31, available for re-appropriation in the next year's Supplemental Budget or ABYIP.
